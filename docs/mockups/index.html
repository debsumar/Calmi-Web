Decision basis

Market archetypes: Oura informed the calm hero and personal-baseline tone; Supabase/OpenAI informed the console shell; OpenAI/Oura account settings informed the ledger ordering.

Quota/KPI practice: Vercel and Dropbox informed exact bounded meters and reset dates; Material 3 and Google One informed the single featured ring; OpenAI/GitHub informed labelled trend context.

Security UX: Google Account and Notion informed read-only personal rows and grouped settings; Apple informed device/session review; OWASP, NIST, and WCAG informed re-auth cues, masked keys, no password values, status text, and focus treatment.
