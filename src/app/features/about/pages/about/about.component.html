Why We Built Calmi?

Mental wellness often feels overwhelming. Many platforms focus on a single solution—meditation, therapy, or habit tracking; leaving people to navigate their journey alone.

Calmi was created to bring everything together in one thoughtful experience. By combining mood tracking, guided journaling, sleep support, AI-powered reflections, and access to licensed psychologists, we aim to help people build healthier emotional habits at their own pace.

What Drives Us?

Every decision we make is guided by a simple belief: mental wellness should feel supportive, not overwhelming.
