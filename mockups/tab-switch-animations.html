Fluid tab switch — 5 variants

Click the tabs in each bar. Same 5 nav items as the real topbar. A–C are motion treatments; D–E keep the bar fully opaque and make the sliding indicator a lens that magnifies the label under it. Pick one and I'll wire it into app.topbar.ts using Calmi tokens.

Variant A — Liquid pill

A soft filled pill travels to the new tab and squashes horizontally while moving, then settles with a light overshoot. Reads as one continuous object rather than a jump.

Feels: calm, premium, product-like. Risk: the fill competes with the topbar glass; needs a low-opacity brand tint to stay quiet.

Variant B — Gooey underline

The underline behaves like liquid: a trailing blob lags behind the leading one, the two stretch apart mid-travel and merge back together on arrival, via an SVG blur + contrast filter.

Feels: playful, distinctive, most "fluid" of the three. Risk: the SVG filter repaints on every frame — heaviest option on low-end mobile.

Variant C — Elastic ink

The underline's leading edge leaves first, so the line stretches to bridge both tabs, then the trailing edge catches up and it contracts into place. A faint ripple blooms under the new tab on arrival.

Feels: closest to the current underline, cheapest to ship, very readable direction of travel. Risk: subtle — least "wow" of the three.

Variant D — Magnifier bubble

Opaque bar, no background trickery. The sliding indicator is a glass bubble that magnifies the label underneath it — the active tab's text bulges as the bubble arrives, with a specular dome on top and a soft rim shadow.

Feels: physical, like a lens resting on the nav. Cost: cheap — one transform, no blur, no SVG filter. Trick: the bubble carries its own scaled copy of the nav row, so nothing behind it has to be translucent.

Variant E — Water droplet lens

Same magnifying bubble, but it behaves like a water droplet: stronger magnification, squash and stretch while travelling, a faint chromatic fringe on the magnified text, and the whole lens ripples through turbulence mid-move before settling clean.

Feels: the most alive of the set. Cost: turbulence runs only while travelling — the class is removed on arrival, so there is zero idle repaint.

My read for Calmi

  • Variant D (magnifier bubble) is my pick now that the bar is opaque — it gives the glass/lens feel with none of the backdrop-filter cost, and it works on any background because the bubble supplies its own magnified text.
  • Variant E (water droplet) is D with personality: stronger zoom, squash-and-stretch, chromatic fringe and a turbulence ripple gated to travel only.
  • Variant C (elastic ink) stays the minimal-risk choice if you'd rather not touch the label rendering at all.
  • A for an app-shell pill, B for the gooey underline.

How the lens works: the bubble is opaque and contains a duplicate of the nav row, offset by its own position and scaled about its centre. The real label under it is set to transparent so nothing double-prints. That means the magnified copy must stay in sync with the real row — so if you pick D or E, the Angular version needs the label list in one place feeding both the buttons and the lens copy.

All five respect prefers-reduced-motion (bubble jumps instantly, no squash, no wobble). Tabs are real buttons with role="tablist", aria-selected and arrow-key support.
