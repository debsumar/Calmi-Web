Rumi voice mode — orb overlay variants

ChatGPT-style voice mode: tapping the voice control raises a circular animated orb over the chat window. It stays until you end the call, and its motion changes with the turn — listening, thinking, speaking. Pick a variant and a state below, or hit Simulate conversation to watch the turns cycle.

A Aurora blob · recommended

Blurred brand-gradient layers drift behind a glossy core; the core breathes slowly while listening and pumps harder while speaking. Closest to ChatGPT‑4o voice mode, and the drift reads as "alive" without looking like a loading spinner. Pure CSS — three blurred radial gradients plus one rotation.

B Sonar rings

Solid orb emitting three staggered expanding rings; ring cadence speeds up on speaking. Clearest signal that audio is flowing, and it reuses the ripple language already in the topbar ink indicator. Risk: rings can read as "recording" or even as an error pulse.

C Morphing blob

Organic border-radius morph plus slow rotation — a squishy liquid mass. Most characterful and least generic, best fit for a calm wellness product. Watch out: continuous morphing can feel restless during long listens, so it wants the slowest tempo of the set.

D Siri dot ring

Sixteen dots on a ring, radius and opacity modulated per dot with a stagger. Reads unmistakably as voice, and maps beautifully onto real amplitude data later — each dot can take a frequency bin from an AnalyserNode. Heaviest DOM of the five (16 nodes).

E Mascot in glass orb

The Rumi logo inside a glass sphere: breathes while listening, tilts while thinking, nods while speaking. Strongest brand payoff and the friendliest read, since the character is already your chat identity. Least abstract, so it can feel more toy-like than the others.

Shared behaviour

All five keep the same shell: overlay sits inside the chat panel with a blurred scrim so context stays visible behind it, a live status line, a caption area for the running transcript, and Mute / End. Nothing dismisses on scrim tap — the call ends only via End, per your requirement. Every variant degrades to a static pose under prefers-reduced-motion.

Colours come from src/tailwind.css (--brand, --brand-light, --brand-deep, --coral) so nothing here implies a new palette value. Icons are the Lucide set already registered in app.config.ts — the trigger uses audio-lines, not a mic, since this is a spoken conversation rather than dictation. States are driven by the classes .is-listening, .is-thinking, .is-speaking on the overlay, which is the same shape an Angular signal would drive.
